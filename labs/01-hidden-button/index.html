Lab 01 · The button that won't click

Mission: The "Save" button below does nothing when clicked. There are no console errors, and the click handler is correct. Find out why, and make the button work without editing this file.

Not saved yet.

Hint 1Use the element picker (Cmd + Shift + C) and hover over the button. Which element gets highlighted?
Hint 2Check the picked element's position, inset and z-index in the Styles pane.
Solution

An invisible div.promo (fully transparent background, position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 5) covers the whole stage, so it receives every click.

Fix it live in any of these ways: select .promo and press Delete; add pointer-events: none to it; or lower its z-index below the button's.

Real-world version: cookie banners, invisible modals that didn't close, and tooltips left behind after an animation.
